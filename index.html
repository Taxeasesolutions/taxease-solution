FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the deadline for filing Income Tax Return for Tax Year 2026?

The standard deadline for Individuals and AOPs to file their annual Income Tax Return for Tax Year 2026 is 30 September 2026, unless extended by the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR).

Why do I need an NTN (National Tax Number)?

An NTN registers you as an active taxpayer with the FBR. It is mandatory for filing tax returns, purchasing/registering high-value property or vehicles, opening business bank accounts, and reducing withholding tax rates across banking and transaction channels.

How can Tax Ease Solution help freelancers and IT export businesses?

We provide full tax planning to claim available export exemptions/concessional tax rates, setup sole proprietorships or SECP SMC-Private Limited entities, track foreign currency remittances, and maintain compliance with FBR and SBP regulations.
