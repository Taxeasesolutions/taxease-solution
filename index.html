FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the Tax Year 2026 return due?

FBR currently lists 30 September as the normal due date for individuals and AOPs. Companies generally have a 31 December due date, while companies with a special tax year have a 30 September due date.

Do you provide NTN registration?

Yes. We provide assistance with taxpayer registration and related documentation and compliance requirements.

Do you register companies with SECP?

Yes. We provide company incorporation and post-incorporation corporate compliance assistance.

Do you provide accounting services?

Yes. Services can include bookkeeping, ledger maintenance, bank reconciliation, financial statements and management reporting.

Can you help with an FBR notice?

We provide professional assistance with notices, documentation, responses and audit-related support subject to the nature of the matter.

Do you work with freelancers?

Yes. We provide tax registration, return preparation, documentation, accounting and compliance assistance for freelancers and digital businesses.
